How the params behave

  • ?save= replaces your save on every load, so refreshing gives you the same day to roll again. ?save=mine puts your own save back.
  • ?rolls= queues results in local storage, one per throw, and they stay until used. Leftovers carry into the next tab, which is how a stray 20 shows up later. The 3D die still lands on a random face; only the result is replaced.
  • Queued rolls are clamped to your die (a 20 on a d6 becomes 6), and with advantage or disadvantage both dice show the queued value.
  • ?condition= and ?season= only last while they're in the URL. A season can't be switched off by URL; turn off seasonal tables in Settings for that.
  • On the table, press ` or tap the Dev chip in the corner to come back here. None of this exists in production builds.
